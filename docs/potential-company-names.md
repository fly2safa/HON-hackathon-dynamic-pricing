## Honeywell Ride-Sharing Division – Name Ideas

Assumed division context: Honeywell launches a ride-sharing / mobility division (similar to Uber/Lyft) and wants the brand name to reference Honeywell.

### Candidate division names (with HONEY)

- **HoneyGo** – Warm, friendly, and memorable; combines "Honey" with "Go" for movement. Sweet branding that's approachable and global.
- **HoneyRide** – Clear ride-sharing service with a friendly, inviting feel; easy to remember and pronounce.
- **HoneyMove** – Emphasizes smooth, efficient mobility; suggests sweetness in the experience and can expand to other transport modes.
- **HoneyFlow** – Evokes smooth, seamless traffic flow; suggests an optimized, pleasant ride experience.
- **HoneyRoute** – Combines efficient routing with a friendly brand; suggests the sweetest path to your destination.
- **HoneyWheels** – Fun and approachable; great for consumer branding with a playful edge.
- **HoneyDrive** – Direct and friendly; works well for both ride-sharing and potential autonomous vehicle services.
- **HoneyLane** – Suggests dedicated, optimized routes; modern and memorable.
- **HoneyTrip** – Emphasizes the journey; friendly and travel-focused.
- **HoneyCommute** – Professional yet approachable; good for daily commuter focus.

### Candidate division names (with HON)

- **HonGo** – Short, punchy, easy to say; clearly about "going" / movement and works globally.
- **HonRide** – Very clear what the service is; simple and professional.
- **HonMove** – Emphasizes smart mobility and movement, and can expand to other modes (scooters, shuttles, etc.).
- **HonLoop** – Modern and techy; suggests continuous movement, connected routes, and an intelligent network.
- **HonRoute** – Conveys routing, navigation, and efficiency; fits well with Honeywell's engineering/aviation image.
- **HonFlow** – Suggests smooth, optimized traffic and trip flow.
- **HonOrbit** – More futuristic; evokes networks, circular routes, and coverage zones.
- **HonFleet** – Sounds enterprise-grade; good if the focus includes B2B, fleet optimization, or corporate transport.
- **HonHop** – Friendly and casual; good for a consumer-facing, approachable brand.
- **HonShare** – Directly reflects ride-sharing and shared mobility.

### Recommended primary name for hackathon use

For your hackathon pitch and project materials, the **recommended division name** is:

**HoneyGo** – "Honeywell HoneyGo: Intelligent Ride-Sharing Platform"

This name combines the warmth and memorability of "Honey" with the action-oriented "Go", creating a brand that's both approachable and dynamic. It maintains a clear connection to Honeywell while establishing its own friendly, consumer-focused identity.

**Alternative top choices:**
- **HoneyRide** – If you want maximum clarity about the service
- **HonGo** – If you prefer a shorter, more tech-focused brand

Use the other options as backup or for product/feature naming variants if needed.
